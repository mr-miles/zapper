Plan and research: starting a repair café in a small UK town

Ada, we're thinking of helping a small UK town (about 15,000 people) start a repair café: a monthly event where volunteers fix residents' broken things for free. Before we commit, we want to know what it takes and whether it works.

Please:

1. Call budget() and ask me for a budget that fits: a research manager and up to two researchers.
2. Hire a research manager to plan and run the research. The questions we care about: what a repair café needs to start and keep going (venue, volunteers, insurance and liability, equipment, funding); what the evidence says about their impact (items repaired, waste avoided, community effects); and what makes them fail or last.
3. When it's done, review the pack and report back to me with the answer in a few sentences, the main risks, what it cost, and the pack attached.

Done means: a research plan on the drive; one pack of Markdown documents (an index, a document per question, and numbered references) that answers each question or says what couldn't be established and why; every non-obvious claim cited; and facts kept apart from judgement.
